Definitions & caveats: Market capacity is the sum of Total Sales Quantity in the uploaded dataset, not independently verified total-market sales. I.H.S = brand SO ÷ market SO in the same geography/month. Sales values and ASP are parsed from strings with spaces and commas. Stores use Store Code + Store Name as a composite identifier. "New" means first observed SO in the uploaded history; "recovered" means active again after an inactive previous month. Haier-display I.H.S universe is a annual cohort of stores with Haier Display Status = "Display" in the latest available month, and includes sales of ALL brands at those stores across selected historical months. FFTLs segmentation requires an FFTLs field in the uploaded JSON; absent data is labeled Unknown. PS Shop and Key Shop are not available in this schema. "Callcenter" and other non-geographic channels remain as their own area rather than being assigned to a physical region. No data leaves your browser; Plotly is loaded from a CDN.
